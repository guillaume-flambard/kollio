## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explore distinguishes imported material from shared reasoning
The Explore section SHALL let eligible members import material, inspect its author, origin and revision history, and promote a selected excerpt. It SHALL state that imported material is not shared reasoning until a human confirms the resulting Contribution.

#### Scenario: A member imports and promotes material
- **WHEN** a member imports material and promotes an excerpt in Explore
- **THEN** the material's provenance and revision remain visible
- **AND** the promoted Contribution is shown under its appropriate confirmation state
