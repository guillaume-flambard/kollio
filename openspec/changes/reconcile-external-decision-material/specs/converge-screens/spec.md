## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Converge reveals source lineage without replacing reasoning
The Converge section SHALL identify a source-backed Contribution and offer its cited excerpt and material revision without rendering raw material as a second map node.

#### Scenario: A member follows source lineage from the map
- **WHEN** a member selects a source-backed Contribution in Converge
- **THEN** the cited excerpt and source revision are reachable
- **AND** the map continues to show the Contribution, not a duplicate raw-material node
