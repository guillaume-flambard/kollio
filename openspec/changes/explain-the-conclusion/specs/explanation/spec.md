# Explanation surface

## Basis

Each factor in the verdict shows its basis. An unknown factor shows, in
its place of score, the missing evidence the engine named.

## Contradictions

When the analysis contradicts a stated objective or constraint, the panel
lists each entry with a translated target label and the engine's prose.

## Locale

All labels come from the translation catalogs; the engine's prose is shown
as returned, in the locale the analysis was requested in.
