## 1. Prepare the adoption inquiry

- [ ] 1.1 Add the six adoption questions to the private pilot acceptance record; verify each question has an evidence field and the allowed conclusions: supported, unsupported, unobserved, or inconclusive.
- [ ] 1.2 Define the observation window and facilitator role before the pilot begins; verify the record distinguishes participant-initiated actions from facilitated actions.
- [ ] 1.3 Prepare pseudonymized participant labels and content-free evidence references; verify no decision text, source material, personal identifier, credential, or prompt is recorded.

## 2. Observe the decision workflow

- [ ] 2.1 Record the event that led the team to start a Decision Space and its prior alternative workflow; verify the trigger is classified as clear, late, or unclear.
- [ ] 2.2 Record whether participants could identify the next responsible action at each blocked moment; verify next-action clarity is separated from general satisfaction.
- [ ] 2.3 Record each material disagreement and its outcome; verify factual claims, assumptions, and authority decisions are distinguished when participants identify them.

## 3. Debrief durable value and trust

- [ ] 3.1 Ask participants for concrete value observed from the completed or stopped loop; verify estimates of time saved or decision quality are not recorded as facts without participant evidence.
- [ ] 3.2 Ask whether a prior learning would be useful in a later decision and whether the participant knows how to retrieve their decision record; verify memory expectation and portability trust are captured separately.
- [ ] 3.3 Classify each of the six adoption angles as supported, unsupported, unobserved, or inconclusive; verify no unobserved angle is reported as validated.

## 4. Turn evidence into bounded follow-up

- [ ] 4.1 Give each finding a disposition: follow-up OpenSpec change for recurring or milestone-blocking friction, explicit deferral, or no change for a non-reproduced finding; verify isolated feature requests do not expand the roadmap.
- [ ] 4.2 Complete `acceptance.md` with the evidence table, privacy boundary, and a conclusion limited to the pilot sample; verify the table links to the real-decision-loop validation where loop-completion evidence is reused.
- [ ] 4.3 Run `openspec validate validate-decision-workflow-adoption --strict` and verify the planning artifacts are structurally valid.
