## Why

Kollio can demonstrate a complete decision loop without yet proving that a team knows when to start one, can resolve disagreement through it, recognizes its value afterwards, and trusts the product enough to keep its decision memory there. These are adoption and product-positioning risks that feature completion alone cannot answer.

## What Changes

- Define a structured pilot inquiry for the decision-start trigger, coordination of waiting work, disagreement governance, non-intrusive memory, economic value, and portability trust.
- Require each inquiry to produce observed evidence, a bounded conclusion, and a disposition rather than a speculative feature request.
- Define a decision rule for follow-up changes: only a repeated or milestone-blocking friction becomes a proposed product change; an isolated preference is recorded but does not expand scope.
- Keep the inquiry content-free and privacy-preserving, using pseudonymized roles and debrief notes that omit decision material.
- Coordinate with `validate-real-decision-loop` by using its real pilot as evidence, without duplicating its loop-completion scenarios.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `decision-workflow-adoption`: evidence and decision criteria for whether teams initiate, coordinate, govern, trust, and reuse the private decision workflow.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

This change adds a pilot observation protocol and acceptance evidence only. It does not add notifications, analytics, memory recommendations, exports, or integration providers. Any feature justified by the findings must be proposed in a separate OpenSpec change with its own behavioral contract.
