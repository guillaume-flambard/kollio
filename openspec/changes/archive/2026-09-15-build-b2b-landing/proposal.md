## Why

Kollio now has a working product foundation, but its public landing page still reads like a generic early scaffold. The first market is private B2B teams, so the page must explain how a team moves an initiative from an initial signal through validation to a documented decision.

## What Changes

- Reframe the landing page around private team workspaces and traceable decisions.
- Show an interactive product preview organized as Signal, Validation, and Decision.
- Add clear enterprise reassurance for privacy, permissions, and controlled AI.
- Preserve French and English copy, authentication entry points, responsive behavior, reduced motion, and the approved Living Canvas visual language.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `b2b-product-landing`: Explains Kollio's team workflow and provides clear paths into a workspace or product demonstration.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Replaces the public Nuxt home page and extends its translation catalogs.
- Adds a reusable initiative-flow preview component and focused UI regression coverage.
