# Golden-path responsive and states

The golden path SHALL hold at the widths a member uses, with no horizontal
overflow and the primary control reachable.

- **RS-01** The initiative detail with its Experiments loop renders at 375 and
  768 without horizontal overflow and the experiment rows reachable.
- **RS-02** The workspace onboarding wizard and its enrichment forms render at
  375 and 768 without horizontal overflow (the phone-width overflow of the
  two-column create forms is fixed).
- **RS-03** The deposit form and its running analysis narration fit a phone
  width with no overflow.
- **RS-04** Every golden-path screen keeps its empty, loading and error states;
  the responsive guard does not replace them.
