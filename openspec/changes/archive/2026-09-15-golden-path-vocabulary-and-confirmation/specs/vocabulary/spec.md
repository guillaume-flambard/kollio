# Golden-path vocabulary and confirmation

The golden path SHALL speak the product's vocabulary and acknowledge the moment
a result becomes company memory.

- **VOC-01** A proposal line is called an "Alternative", never a "branch", in the
  timeline and the proposal hint, in FR and EN.
- **VOC-02** An accepted proposal reads "Accepted", not "Merged"; the in-flight
  analysis reads "Analysis in progress", not "running".
- **VOC-03** Confirming a learning shows "Added to the company memory" as a
  discrete, reduced-motion-safe state on the confirmed learning, not a silent swap.
- **VOC-04** No technical jargon (branch, merged, commit, run, workflow, repo,
  embedding, vector) appears in the visible golden-path strings; the design
  guard for tokens still passes.
