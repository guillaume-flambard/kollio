## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Authenticated screens share one visual system
Every authenticated Kollio screen SHALL use the canonical Living Canvas tokens, shared theme, Kollio-owned SVG iconography and approved Aubergine brand mark.

#### Scenario: A security surface is rendered
- **WHEN** a member opens provider, audit or retention controls
- **THEN** the surface SHALL inherit the same visual language as the Decision Space
- **AND** it SHALL NOT introduce raw visual values, third-party icons or a separate palette.
