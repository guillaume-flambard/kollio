## Findings

1. **Contrast: pending badge below AA.** `.settings-pending`
   (`settings.vue:765`) paints `color: var(--ui-warning)` on
   `color-mix(in srgb, var(--ui-warning) 22%, transparent)` at
   `--kollio-text-micro` (11px). Measured against the light surface that is
   3.04:1, under the 4.5:1 AA floor for normal-size text. The dark block in
   `tokens.css` overrides `--ui-success` and `--ui-error` but not
   `--ui-warning`, so the same token has no dark-theme value either.
2. **Error state is not tokenised as an error.** `.settings-error`
   (`settings.vue:753`) uses `background: var(--ui-bg-elevated)` and the
   inherited text colour; unlike `.deposit-error` (`deposit.vue:189`), it never
   applies `--ui-error`. The two `role="alert"` blocks (load failure and action
   failure) therefore read as neutral notes, and the alert styling is
   inconsistent across the workspace.
3. **State pill contrast.** `.settings-state` (`settings.vue:759`) pairs
   `--ui-text-muted` text with `--ui-bg-muted`/`--ui-bg-accented`; measured on
   `--ui-bg-accented` that is 4.30:1 at `--kollio-text-caption`, marginally
   under AA.
