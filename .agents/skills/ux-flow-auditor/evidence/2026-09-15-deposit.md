## Findings

1. **Token discipline: literal color and unit values in `deposit.vue`.** The
   submit button (`.deposit-submit`, `deposit.vue:186`) sets `color: white`
   instead of a semantic token; the same literal appears in
   `.ideas-create-action` (`index.vue:218`). Field and verdict blocks also use
   literal `12px` radii (`deposit.vue:181,185`) instead of
   `--kollio-radius-md`, and literal `.9rem`/`.875rem`/`.78rem` sizes instead of
   `--kollio-text-small`/`--kollio-text-caption`/`--kollio-text-micro`. These
   bypass the `packages/ui/src/tokens.css` scale.
2. **Missing terminal state in analysis polling.** `pollAnalysis`
   (`deposit.vue:59-77`) stops after 20 polls (about 8s) with a bare
   `stopPolling()` and never leaves the running narration: there is no timeout,
   retry or error surface. A slow or stalled analysis leaves the user on
   `ideas.deposit.narration.*` forever with no recovery control. No scenario
   covers the poll-exhaustion path.

## Verdict

All five deposit flows AUDITED for fr and en desktop behaviour. Finding 2 is a
gap, not a regression: the tested paths pass.
