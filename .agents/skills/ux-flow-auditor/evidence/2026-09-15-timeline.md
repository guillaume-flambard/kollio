## Findings

1. **Invalid `<time datetime>` value.** `IterationTimeline.vue:56` binds
   `:datetime="entry.dateLabel"`, but `dateLabel` is the human, localised display
   string produced by `TimelineFormatters.formatDate` (`app/utils/timeline.ts`),
   not an ISO 8601 value. A localised string such as "1 septembre 2026" in the
   machine-readable `datetime` attribute is invalid HTML and defeats date
   parsing by assistive tech and crawlers that read `<time datetime>`.
2. **No token or locale defect observed** on the rendered labels.

## Verdict

TIMELINE-01 AUDITED for fr and en. Finding 1 is a semantics defect the scenario
does not assert.
