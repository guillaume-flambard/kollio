# Kollio Product Context

## Product

Kollio is a bilingual web product that turns early ideas into evidence-backed, versioned collective work. An idea is a living deposit with a durable history, explicit constraints, contributions, decisions, and a team forming around it.

## Users

- Idea owners who need to expose, test, and refine an idea.
- Contributors who add evidence, proposals, skills, and execution capacity.
- Observers who follow progress before they decide to contribute.
- Private workspace members who browse and evaluate imported or internal ideas.

## Core jobs

1. Understand an idea and its current maturity quickly.
2. See the strongest constraints and the evidence behind them.
3. Decide what contribution or experiment should happen next.
4. Preserve the history of proposals, decisions, and outcomes.
5. Find complementary people and ideas across French and English.

## Positioning and moat

Kollio is an idea collaboration network built around evidence and iteration rather than a static idea directory. Its defensibility comes from the accumulated graph connecting people, contributions, decisions, constraints, experiments, and outcomes. Multilingual semantic matching makes that graph useful across French and English content.

## Product principles

- Make the next useful action obvious.
- Treat constraints as explained evidence, not as decorative scores.
- Keep iteration history legible and append-only.
- Make collaboration visible at the point where it helps an idea advance.
- Use AI to clarify, compare, and structure decisions while keeping outputs inspectable.
- Preserve user content in its original language and translate it on demand.
- Keep private workspaces isolated by default.

## Current scope

The current foundation supports authentication, private workspace membership, idea browsing, and idea detail. The next product slices add idea submission, constraint analysis, versioned iteration, team formation, exploration, and profiles. Investment features remain outside the current scope.

## Experience constraints

- French and English are first-class interface locales.
- The interface is light-first, accessible, responsive, and keyboard usable.
- The primary accent is blue; dark tokens may exist without driving the default experience.
- The Nuxt application consumes shared semantic tokens and generated API contracts.
- Product screens should favor information hierarchy and purposeful density over generic card grids.
